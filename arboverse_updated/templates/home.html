ARBOVERSE: An interactive arbovirus data platform

Providing open-source data visualizations to virologists, ecologists, epidemiologists, public health officials, and citizen scientists

OUR GOAL

To provide comprehensive knowledge and data on arboviruses through a user-friendly, open-access platform

Contact us for collaboration

OUR WORK

We have synthesized a century of arbovirus research into an interactive resource, integrating global knowledge and data visualizations within a broad ecological context

ARBOCARD

Summary information on arboviruses and their associated diseases

ARBOVECTOR

Concise information on arbovirus vectors

ARBOMAP

Interactive, high-resolution maps to investigate arboviruses in an ecological context

ARBODATA

Comprehensive datasets connecting arboviruses, vectors, and ecological factors
