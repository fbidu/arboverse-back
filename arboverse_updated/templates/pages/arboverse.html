    Aedes-borne virus transmission risk expansion

    Fuction

    Displays the global expansion and redistribution of Aedes-borne virus transmission risk under various climate change scenarios

    Spatial resolution

    5-minute spatial resolution (~10 km2 at the equator)

    Geographic coverage

    Global

    Update frequency

    Not updated (Static model)

    Temporal coverage

    Representative Concentration Pathways (RCP) 2.6 and 8.5 using the HadGEM2-ES model

    License
    CC0 1.0
    Overview

    This dataset illustrates the number of months per year in which temperature suitability allows Aedes-borne virus transmission by comparing baseline conditions (1960–1990) with future projections for 2050 and 2080. The original study evaluated four scenarios (RCP 2.6, 4.5, 6.0, and 8.5), ARBOVERSE displays the RCP 2.6 and RCP 8.5 models. Please consult the original source for detailed methodologies and specific data limitations

    Modification

    The layer utilizes the “roma” scientific color map developed by Crameri et al. (2020; PMID: 33116149)

    Source

    Ryan et al., PLoS Negl Trop Dis. 2019 (PMID: 30921321)

    Köppen-Geiger classification

    Fuction

    Displays present-day and future Köppen-Geiger climate classifications

    Spatial resolution

    0.0083° spatial resolution (~1 km2 at the equator)

    Geographic coverage

    Global

    Update frequency

    Not updated (Static model)

    Temporal coverage

    Present-day (1980–2016) and projected future conditions (2071–2100)

    License
    CC BY 4.0
    Overview

    This dataset illustrates the Köppen-Geiger climate classification, which categorizes global climates into tropical, arid, temperate, continental, and polar, and further divides them into 29 subclasses. The present-day map was derived from an ensemble of four high-resolution, topographically corrected climatic maps. The future map (2071–2100) was generated by superimposing projected climate change anomalies from 32 models onto the high-resolution baseline. Please consult the original source for detailed methodologies and specific data limitations

    Modification

    The layer utilizes the “hawaii” scientific color map developed by Crameri et al. (2020; PMID: 33116149)

    Source

    Beck et al. Sci Data. 2018 (PMID: 30375988)
