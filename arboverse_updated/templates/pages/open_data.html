ARBOVERSE open-access data

Accelerating global health research in arbovirus

ARBOVERSE is a strong advocate of open and free access. All components of our work are available for use by other groups on an open access or unrestricted basis.

About the data

ARBOVERSE serves as a centralized repository for global arbovirus data. While the raw data is sourced from existing public records and institutional databases, ARBOVERSE provides value through data harmonization—unifying disparate formats into a single, standardized, and machine-readable platform.

Attribution

Our arbovirus data and maps are available to all users under The Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). We ask that any use of these data provides the correct citation.
