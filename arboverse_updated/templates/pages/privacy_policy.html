ARBOVERSE Privacy policy

Purpose

This Privacy Policy is to inform users of our website of the following:

  1. Cookie policy;
  2. Data collection and usage;
  3. Data storage and retention;
  4. How we protect your data
  5. Opt-out options and disabling cookies;
  6. Contact and communication; and
  7. External links.

This Privacy Policy applies in addition to the terms and conditions of our websites.

Consent

By using our website users agree that they consent to:

  1. The conditions set out in this Privacy Policy; and
  2. The collection, use, and retention of the data listed herein.

Cookie policy

A cookie is a small file stored on a user’s hard drive by a website to collect data relating to browsing habits. You can choose to be notified each time a cookie is transmitted or disable cookies entirely in your browser; however, this may affect the quality of your user experience.

This website uses a cookie control system allowing users, on their first visit, to allow or disallow the use of cookies on their device. This complies with legislative requirements for websites to obtain explicit consent before reading or placing cookie files.

Data collected

We only collect data that helps us achieve the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will not collect additional data without prior notification.

Data collected automatically via Google Analytics

When you visit ARBOVERSE, Google Analytics may automatically collect:

  1. Timestamp of the visit;
  2. Whether you are a returning visitor;
  3. The referring site or webpage;
  4. Pages visited within ARBOVERSE; and
  5. Time spent on each webpage.

These data do not contain personal information; however, your IP address is used to determine your approximate geographic location. Google stores this information on servers in the United States and may transfer it to third parties where required by law or for processing on Google’s behalf.

Data retention

User data collected for statistical purposes will be stored for fourteen (14) days. Users will be notified if data is retained beyond this period.

Data protection

To ensure your security, we use robust browser encryption and store data on servers in secure facilities. Data is only accessible to authorized personnel bound by strict confidentiality agreements. We take all reasonable precautions, but the inherent nature of the internet means we cannot guarantee absolute security beyond what is reasonably practical.

Opt-out & disabling cookies

To provide visitors with more choice, you may use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on http://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=None This prevents the JavaScript (ga.js) from sharing visit information with Google Analytics. For more details, see the Google Analytics Privacy Advice https://policies.google.com/privacy Users may also restrict or block cookies through their browser settings (typically accessed via the F1 key or "Help" menu).

Contact & communication

Users contacting this website do so at their own discretion and provide personal details at their own risk. Your personal information is kept private and stored securely until it is no longer required, in accordance with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 (UK GDPR). Any information submitted is used solely to provide further information about our services or to assist in answering your queries. Your details are not passed on to third parties.

External links

Although we aim to include only safe and relevant external links, users should adopt a policy of caution. The ARBOVERSE cannot guarantee the contents of externally linked websites. Users click on external links at their own risk; ARBOVERSE and its owners cannot be held liable for any damages caused by visiting external links.

Modifications

This Privacy Policy may be amended periodically to maintain legal compliance and reflect changes to our data collection process. Updates will be indicated by the “Effective Date” at the top of this page.

Contact information

If you have questions, concerns, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at wmdesouza@uky.edu
